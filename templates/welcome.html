Platform Overview

UrbanHRPartners Enterprise Suite centralizes the management of client relationships, human resources operations, recruiting, onboarding, safety compliance, finance, marketing, scheduling, inventory, and executive decision support through XIOMY AI.

This environment is structured to provide a unified command center for business growth, workforce performance, compliance visibility, and operational control.

Core Modules

  • CRM – Client Relationship & Revenue Engine
  • HRIS – Human Resources Information System
  • ATS – Applicant Tracking System
  • Orientation – Onboarding and readiness workflows
  • SG-SST – Safety and compliance management
  • Finance – Revenue, invoicing, and projections
  • Inventory – Assets, tracking, and stock control
  • Marketing – Leads, campaigns, and outreach
  • Calendar – Scheduling and event coordination
  • XIOMY AI – Executive intelligence assistant
