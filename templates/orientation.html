Orientation Workflow Overview

The onboarding engine is designed to control activation readiness in structured stages. A typical enterprise sequence uses hiring conversion from ATS, employee creation in HRIS, orientation scheduling, policy acknowledgement, payroll readiness, SG-SST induction, system access, and equipment assignment before the employee is considered fully operational.

1. Hiring Conversion

Candidate application is converted into an employee record through the ATS hiring flow. That employee then becomes eligible for the orientation checklist sequence.

2. Orientation Scheduling

New hires are assigned to sessions and administrative onboarding events for welcome, compliance, role readiness, and company policy introduction.

3. Readiness Completion

Checklist items track setup steps such as payroll enrollment, policy acceptance, safety briefing, systems access, and department-specific activation actions.

Create Orientation Session

Schedule official onboarding sessions for welcome, role briefing, payroll explanation, policy review, team introduction, and compliance support.

Use this for official welcome sessions, induction blocks, onboarding workshops, compliance briefings, and cross-functional introduction events.

Create Orientation Checklist Item

Build onboarding actions tied to an employee, application, session, and responsible user. This is the task control layer for enterprise onboarding completion.

Create Policy Acknowledgement

Register employee acknowledgement of corporate policies, conduct standards, role obligations, operational rules, and onboarding compliance documents.

This record supports compliance traceability and onboarding evidence capture.

Create Asset Assignment

Assign company assets to new employees and connect the assignment to inventory movement control during onboarding and operational activation.
