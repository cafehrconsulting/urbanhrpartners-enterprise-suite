Orientation Executive Purpose

This module operates as the activation bridge between recruiting and workforce entry.

Candidate-to-Employee Transition
Ensure selected candidates move from ATS into a structured onboarding path without losing documentation, status, or readiness context.
Policy & Compliance Readiness
Track acknowledgements, safety-related steps, and first-day procedural obligations before employee activation.
Operational Activation
Coordinate equipment, task completion, and orientation workflow to support smoother entry into HRIS and daily operations.

Onboarding Leadership Priorities

Controls that should remain visible to leadership and XIOMY.

  • Do not activate new hires with incomplete documentation or unresolved policy gaps.
  • Keep checklist completion visible and actionable before day one.
  • Ensure assets and equipment are ready before orientation closure.
  • Bridge orientation results to HRIS activation and SG-SST readiness.
  • Prepare this workflow for future XIOMY onboarding insights and alerts.
