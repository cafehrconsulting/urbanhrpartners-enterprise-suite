ATS Executive Purpose

This module is designed to function as the talent acquisition and hiring pipeline command center of the platform.

Candidate Lifecycle Management
Manage applicants from first contact through review, selection, interviews, offer progression, and onboarding readiness.
Recruiting Visibility
Keep hiring organized with structured talent records, stage tracking, and better coordination between recruiter action and onboarding flow.
Enterprise Integration
Connect candidate flow with Orientation, HRIS, documentation readiness, equipment assignment, and future analytics.
